/ President and Chief Executive Officer

Nikhil Lalwani

President and Chief Executive Officer
Board Member

Joined ANI in September 2020

Guiding ANI's rare-disease evolution.

Cipla USA InvaGen McKinsey Medtronic Wharton MBA

Mr. Lalwani is ANI's President and Chief Executive Officer and serves on ANI's Board. Before joining ANI, he spent more than eight years at Cipla Ltd., where he held roles including CEO of Cipla USA, CEO of InvaGen, Head of U.S. Strategy, M&A and Integration, and Head of Cipla's Global Respiratory business.

Across those roles, he developed and executed multi-year growth plans for key products and helped lead acquisitions as Cipla entered specialty pharmaceuticals. Earlier in his career, Mr. Lalwani was an Associate Partner with McKinsey & Company serving pharmaceutical and healthcare companies around the world, and an engineer with Medtronic. He holds a B.S. in Electrical Engineering from Georgia Institute of Technology and an M.B.A. from the Wharton School at the University of Pennsylvania.

Stephen P. Carey

Senior Vice President, Finance
Chief Financial Officer

Joined ANI in May 2016

Financial leadership through scale and transformation.

Par Pharmaceutical Schering-Plough PwC 30+ years

Mr. Carey is ANI's Senior Vice President, Finance and Chief Financial Officer. He has more than 30 years of experience as a financial executive, including more than 25 years in the pharmaceutical industry.

Before joining ANI, Mr. Carey held executive financial positions at Par Pharmaceutical Companies, including Senior Vice President, Controller and Principal Accounting Officer. During his tenure, he supported Par through its 2012 leveraged buyout with TPG Capital and its 2015 sale to Endo International. Earlier, he held financial and accounting roles at Schering-Plough and began his career at PricewaterhouseCoopers. He holds a B.S. in Accounting from Montclair State University.

Meredith W. Cook

Senior Vice President
General Counsel and Corporate Secretary

Joined ANI in July 2022

Legal counsel for governance, growth, and strategic transactions.

Amneal Dr. Reddy's Morgan Lewis Tulane J.D.

Ms. Cook is ANI's Senior Vice President, General Counsel and Corporate Secretary. She brings more than 20 years of legal experience spanning corporate governance, strategic transactions, commercial contracts, employment matters, and intellectual property.

Prior to ANI, Ms. Cook served as Vice President and Associate General Counsel for Amneal Pharmaceuticals, where she supported corporate and strategic transactions across business units. She previously served as Global Legal Head, M&A, Business Development and Strategic Transactions for Dr. Reddy's Laboratories and practiced at Morgan, Lewis & Bockius and Vinson & Elkins. She earned her bachelor's degree from Princeton University and her Juris Doctorate from Tulane Law School.

Chief Human Resources Officer

People leadership for a growing pharmaceutical organization.

Novartis A.T. Kearney Reckitt Benckiser McGill

Ms. Davis is ANI's Senior Vice President and Chief Human Resources Officer. She brings more than 20 years of executive leadership experience in human resources, talent management, and organizational development.

Chad Gassert

Senior Vice President
Corporate Development and Strategy

Joined ANI in November 2021

Corporate development shaped by company-building experience.

Novitium Par Pharmaceuticals Sandoz Wyeth

Mr. Gassert joined ANI through the acquisition of Novitium Pharma, where he was Co-Founder and CEO. At Novitium, he led the buildout of a fully integrated generic pharmaceutical company focused on R&D, manufacturing, and distribution of prescription products.

Prior to Novitium, Mr. Gassert held roles of increasing responsibility at Par Pharmaceuticals, including Senior Vice President of Business Development. In that role, he led corporate and business development activities across M&A, partnerships and licensing, portfolio management, project management, and strategic sourcing. Earlier, he worked at Sandoz and Wyeth as a formulation and technical services scientist. He holds a B.S. in Nutritional Science from the University of Delaware.

Ori Gutwerg

Generics

Generics commercial leadership

Generics leadership across portfolio, growth, and business development.

Taro Xiromed US Perrigo Tel Aviv MBA

Mr. Gutwerg brings more than 17 years of experience building and driving growth in pharmaceutical companies. Most recently, he served as Vice President, Head of U.S. Generics Rx at Taro Pharmaceuticals, overseeing a portfolio of nearly 300 generic and branded products.

Christopher Mutz

Senior Vice President
Head of Rare Disease

Joined ANI in February 2021

Rare disease leadership across launch strategy and lifecycle management.

Alexion Merck Rare Disease Cornell MBA

Mr. Mutz joined ANI in February 2021 and serves as the Company's Head of Rare Disease. Prior to joining ANI, he provided consulting services to pharma and biotech companies on pre-launch commercial strategy and lifecycle management focused on rare diseases.

Mr. Mutz has more than 25 years of pharmaceutical industry experience, including eight years at Alexion Pharmaceuticals and 10 years at Merck. At Alexion, he held leadership roles in sales and marketing business units and served as Vice President, Metabolic Business Unit and later Vice President, Neurology Business Unit. While at Merck, he held roles in U.S. Sales, U.S. Marketing, Global Marketing, and Merck's China subsidiary. He holds a B.A. in Biology from the University of Virginia and an M.B.A. from Cornell University.

Muthusamy "Samy" Shanmugam

Head of R&D
COO of New Jersey Operations

Joined ANI Board in November 2021

R&D and New Jersey operations led by formulation expertise.

Novitium Founder Formulation Industrial Pharmacy NY Pharmacist

Mr. Shanmugam is ANI's Head of Research & Development and Chief Operating Officer of New Jersey Operations and a member of ANI's Board. He joined the Board in November 2021 in connection with ANI's acquisition of Novitium Pharma, where he was President and Founder.

Mr. Shanmugam has more than three decades of pharmaceutical industry experience. He began his career as a formulation scientist at Kali Laboratories and grew to become a leading formulation development scientist in the generic drug industry. Prior to co-founding Novitium, he held positions at Par Pharmaceutical and Novel Laboratories. He is also a director and founder of Nuray Chemicals Private Limited, Sthree Chemicals Private Limited, SS Pharma LLC, and Scitus Pharma Services Pvt Ltd. He obtained his M.S. in Industrial Pharmacy from the Tamil Nadu Dr. M.G.R. Medical University and is a registered pharmacist in New York.

Thomas Rowland

Senior Vice President
Head of Established Brands

Joined ANI in November 2021

Established brands leadership with broad pharmaceutical operations depth.

Arbor Ventrus 30+ years Commercial Operations

Thomas Rowland is ANI's Senior Vice President, Head of Established Brands. Prior to joining ANI in November 2021, he was President and Chief Operating Officer of Arbor Pharmaceuticals, a specialty pharmaceutical company with a portfolio across multiple therapeutic areas.

Earlier, Mr. Rowland was Chief Business Officer at Ventrus Biosciences, a development-stage company focused in gastroenterology. He has more than 30 years of experience in pharmaceutical operations, including leading commercial organizations as large as 650 people with revenue up to $500 million. He has also led development, regulatory, quality, and manufacturing functions. Mr. Rowland holds a B.S. in Finance from Metropolitan State University.
