Corporate overview

A diversified U.S. biopharma becoming a rare-disease company.

ANI Pharmaceuticals (Nasdaq: ANIP) develops, manufactures, and commercializes therapeutics for people living with rare and uncommon conditions. We deliver growth through three businesses:

  • Rare Disease. Markets products across ophthalmology, rheumatology, nephrology, neurology, and pulmonology — anchored by Cortrophin® Gel and ILUVIEN®.
  • Generics. R&D expertise, operational depth, and U.S.-based manufacturing across 200+ products.
  • Established Brands. Trusted therapies kept reliably available.
