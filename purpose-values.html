ANI / About / Purpose & Values

Serving patients. Improving lives.

That's the whole sentence. Everything below is how we keep it true.

We make medicines for people who don't have many other options. The patient with multiple sclerosis whose corticosteroid stopped working. The patient with chronic diabetic macular edema watching their vision narrow. The hospital pharmacist who needs a generic to be in stock the first time they call.

Our purpose isn't a poster on the wall. It's the operating instruction.

Our Values

Five values, written as verbs.

01

Patients first

What it means: Every decision answers to one question - does this help a patient?

In practice: When a generic goes on the FDA shortage list, we prioritize the lots. When a rare-disease patient calls our hub, a human picks up.

02

Quality, no asterisks

What it means: cGMP isn't a target. It's the floor.

In practice: Three FDA-inspected facilities. A culture where any operator can stop the line.

03

Plain talk

What it means: Say what you mean. Cite what you know.

In practice: No "industry-leading." No "unprecedented." If we have data, we show the data. If we don't, we say so.

04

Make it, don't just market it

What it means: We are a manufacturer. The mark of pride is what comes off the line.

In practice: Baudette runs solid- and liquid-dose for our own portfolio and partners. East Windsor handles R&D and specialty formulations.

05

Earn the rare in rare disease

What it means: When a condition affects fewer people, the company serving them has to work harder, not less.

In practice: Patient support hubs, savings programs, and HCP MedInfo lines for every rare-disease product we sell.
