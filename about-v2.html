Our story, in chapters.

ANI didn't arrive at rare disease in a single move. We compounded it - chapter by chapter - from a small-town generics maker into a diversified biopharma centered on patients others overlook.

01 2001 - 2020

Generics, made in Baudette.

Founded in 2001, ANI built its reputation on hands-on generics manufacturing in Baudette, Minnesota - a 200,000+ sq ft FDA-inspected facility. Two decades of compounding quality, ANDA-by-ANDA, in a market that rewards consistency over noise.

"Quality wasn't a slogan. It was the floor."
02 2020 - 2022

Cortrophin returns to the U.S. market.

In October 2022, ANI launched Cortrophin Gel - the first new ACTH therapy in 70 years and the only U.S.-manufactured repository corticotropin with a competitive WAC. A four-year regulatory and manufacturing build culminated in a launch that reframed ANI as a rare-disease commercial company.

"A 70-year gap, closed in four."
03 2024 - 2025

Rare-disease portfolio, expanded.

The 2024 acquisition of Alimera Sciences added ILUVIEN and YUTIQ - sustained-release ophthalmic implants - broadening the rare-disease franchise into chronic posterior segment disease. Cortrophin Gel grew 76% year over year in 2025, reaching $400M+ in net revenue.

"From one rare medicine to a portfolio."
04 2026 →

A 90-person specialty deployment.

Mid-2026, ANI deploys a 90-person commercial team for acute gouty arthritis flares - the next branded indication for Cortrophin. 2026 guidance midpoint: $1.085B in net revenue, with rare disease at ~60% of mix and adjusted EBITDA of $275 - $290M.

"Care, codified - and continuing."

Indications & Usage

Cortrophin® Gel (repository corticotropin injection) is indicated as adjunctive therapy for short-term administration in conditions including acute exacerbations of multiple sclerosis, rheumatic disorders, collagen diseases, allergic states, ophthalmic diseases, respiratory diseases, edematous states (induction of diuresis or remission of proteinuria in nephrotic syndrome), and serum sickness.

ILUVIEN® (fluocinolone acetonide intravitreal implant) 0.19 mg is indicated for the treatment of diabetic macular edema in patients who have been previously treated with a course of corticosteroids and did not have a clinically significant rise in intraocular pressure, and for the treatment of chronic non-infectious uveitis affecting the posterior segment of the eye.
