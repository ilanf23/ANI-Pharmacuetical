Heads up

Two minutes on hiring fraud.

We've seen scams that impersonate ANI. Here's what real outreach looks like vs. the warning signs.

Real ANI recruiting
  • Email arrives from the official ANI domain. anipharmaceuticals.com
  • Recruiter has a verified LinkedIn profile linked to ANI.
  • Interviews are scheduled through ANI's official tools.
  • Roles always live on the official jobs site. jobs.anipharmaceuticals.com
Warning signs
  • Email from a free domain claiming ANI affiliation. gmail.com, outlook.com, etc.
  • Any request for payment, banking, or sensitive financial info.
  • Pressure to interview only by SMS, WhatsApp, or Telegram.
  • An offer that skips a recruiter call or written role description.
