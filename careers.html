How to apply

Find the right opening. Talk to a real recruiter.

All official ANI roles are posted through the jobs site. Recruiting communication should come from ANI channels, and role details should be confirmed in writing.

  • Review the current opening and location before applying.
  • Ask your recruiter about team structure, schedule expectations, and benefits eligibility.
  • Use the benefits page for a quick overview before offer conversations.
Heads up

Two minutes on hiring fraud.

We've seen scams that impersonate ANI. Protect yourself before you reply.

Email domain Real ANI recruiters write from @anipharmaceuticals.com only.
Verified profile Real ANI recruiters have verified LinkedIn profiles.
No payment requests We never ask for payment, banking info, or sensitive financial data during hiring.
Official openings All real openings live at jobs.anipharmaceuticals.com.
