About ANI Pharmaceuticals

A diversified biopharmaceutical company building toward a rare-disease future.

ANI Pharmaceuticals (NASDAQ: ANIP) develops, manufactures, and commercializes therapeutics for people living with rare and uncommon conditions - and the healthcare professionals who treat them. Founded in 2001 and headquartered in Baudette, Minnesota since 2010, we operate three FDA-inspected facilities and market a rare-disease franchise built around Cortrophin® Gel and ILUVIEN®.

01 2001 — 2020

Built on quality.

Before rare disease was our headline, made in the USA was our promise.

Founded in 2001 as a U.S.-based manufacturer of generic medicines. In 2010, headquarters relocated to Baudette, Minnesota, consolidating operations around its FDA-inspected facility. Over the next decade: 100+ ANDAs across three plants — the manufacturing backbone for everything that came after.

02 2020 — 2022

A branded inflection.

Bringing Purified Cortrophin® Gel back into a category held by a single supplier for nearly 70 years.

September 2020: Nikhil Lalwani joins as CEO with a mandate to pivot from generics to rare-disease leadership. October 2021: FDA approves ANI's BLA. January 2022: Cortrophin relaunches. By 2025, the franchise reaches $348M (+76% YoY) — validating the bet.

03 2024 — 2025

A two-franchise rare-disease company.

The Alimera Sciences acquisition brought ILUVIEN® into the portfolio.

September 2024: $381M Alimera acquisition closes, bringing ILUVIEN® (fluocinolone acetonide intravitreal implant) for diabetic macular edema. 2025: YUTIQ® unified into ILUVIEN, simplifying the ophthalmology franchise. Two long-acting rare-disease platforms, under one roof.

04 2026 →

What's next.

$1B+ in projected revenue. A 90-person sales team deploys mid-2026.

2026 guidance: $1.055–1.115B net revenue, $275–290M adjusted EBITDA. Cortrophin: $540–575M. ILUVIEN: $78–83M. The acute gouty arthritis flare launch expands Cortrophin's addressable market across 8.3M U.S. adults.
