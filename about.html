Our Story / 2001 — present

From manufacturer to medicine maker.

Two decades of compounding quality — from generic medicines to a rare-disease franchise serving patients others forget.

01
2001–2020

Built on quality.

Before rare disease was our headline, made in the USA was our promise.

Founded in 2001, ANI began as a U.S.-based manufacturer of generic medicines — building a reputation for quality that would become the foundation of everything to come.

In 2010, ANI relocated its headquarters to Baudette, Minnesota, consolidating operations around its FDA-inspected manufacturing facility. Over the next decade, the company grew its portfolio to 100+ ANDAs across three plants, establishing the manufacturing backbone that would later support a branded franchise.

02
2020–2022

A branded inflection.

When we set out to bring Purified Cortrophin® Gel back to the U.S. market, we entered a category that had been a single-supplier monopoly for nearly 70 years.

In September 2020, Nikhil Lalwani joined as CEO with a mandate to transform ANI from a diversified generics company into a rare-disease leader. The first move: bringing Purified Cortrophin® Gel back to market after the FDA approved ANI's BLA in October 2021.

Cortrophin relaunched in January 2022, entering a category that had been a single-supplier monopoly for nearly 70 years. By 2025, the franchise reached $348M in revenue — a 76% year-over-year increase — validating the pivot.

03
2024–2025

Becoming a rare-disease company.

The acquisition of Alimera Sciences brought ILUVIEN® into our portfolio and made us a two-franchise rare-disease company.

In September 2024, ANI completed the $381M acquisition of Alimera Sciences, bringing ILUVIEN® (fluocinolone acetonide intravitreal implant) into the portfolio for the treatment of diabetic macular edema.

By 2025, ANI unified the YUTIQ® brand into ILUVIEN, simplifying the ophthalmology franchise and establishing itself as a two-franchise rare-disease company with differentiated long-acting therapies.

04
2026 →

What's next.

2026 guidance projects $1.055–$1.115B in net revenue and $275–290M adjusted EBITDA. Cortrophin is expected to deliver $540–575M. ILUVIEN targets $78–83M. A 90-person commercial team for acute gouty arthritis flares deploys mid-2026, expanding Cortrophin's addressable market.

Why ANI · The Commercial Argument

The first new ACTH therapy in seven decades.

Cortrophin® Gel re-entered the U.S. market in January 2022 as the first new repository corticotropin injection approved by the FDA in over 70 years. It offers a competitive wholesale acquisition cost, eight FDA-approved indications, and a commercial infrastructure built from the ground up — 90 sales representatives deployed across rheumatology and nephrology.
