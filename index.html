Inside one dose

  1. 01 · Unbox

    Product parts

    Everything in one kit — prefilled syringe, vial, alcohol prep pad, gauze, and sharps container.

    Each pack ships ready for a single subcutaneous dose. No additional supplies to order, no pharmacy trips between refills, and no question about what comes next.

    • 1 prefilled syringe
    • 80 USP units / 0.5 mL vial
    • Sharps container included
  2. 02 · Prep

    Prepare

    Refrigerated storage at 2–8°C. Hands washed. Vial stopper wiped with the alcohol pad.

    Cold-chain integrity is verified at every hand-off, from manufacturing to specialty pharmacy to the patient’s door. Step-by-step instructions ship inside every kit and are mirrored online.

    • 2–8°C cold chain
    • Single-use, sterile
    • Plain-language guide
  3. 03 · Inject

    Use

    Subcutaneous injection at a 90° angle, followed by safe disposal in the included sharps container.

    Most patients self-administer at home in under two minutes. A nurse-led teaching session is available on request, and a clinical support line answers questions between visits.

    • Subcutaneous, 90° angle
    • Self-administered at home
    • Nurse teaching available
  4. 04 · Live

    Live

    Get back to your day. Patient support stays with you between doses, on every prescription.

    Benefits investigation, prior-authorization assistance, and copay programs run in parallel with therapy — so disruptions are caught upstream, not at the pharmacy counter on refill day.

    • Benefits & PA assistance
    • Copay support programs
    • Refill coordination

Indications & Usage

Cortrophin® Gel (repository corticotropin injection) is indicated as adjunctive therapy for short-term administration in conditions including acute exacerbations of multiple sclerosis, rheumatic disorders, collagen diseases, allergic states, ophthalmic diseases, respiratory diseases, edematous states (induction of diuresis or remission of proteinuria in nephrotic syndrome), and serum sickness.

ILUVIEN® (fluocinolone acetonide intravitreal implant) 0.19 mg is indicated for the treatment of diabetic macular edema in patients who have been previously treated with a course of corticosteroids and did not have a clinically significant rise in intraocular pressure, and for the treatment of chronic non-infectious uveitis affecting the posterior segment of the eye.
