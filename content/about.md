## Section — What we do

Three blocks, one paragraph each.

**Rare Disease.** Cortrophin® Gel for the conditions where corticotropin is indicated — multiple sclerosis flares, rheumatoid arthritis, nephrotic syndrome, and more. ILUVIEN® for chronic diabetic macular edema. The franchise spans ophthalmology, rheumatology, nephrology, neurology, and pulmonology.

**Generics.** A 200+ product portfolio across liquid, tablet, capsule, injection, topical, powder, and enema. Made in U.S. facilities. Available to wholesalers, hospitals, and contract partners.

**Established Brands.** Therapies that patients and clinicians have relied on for decades — kept reliably available. We don't sunset them. We supply them.

## Section — How we got here

A short timeline, four moments — not a wall of text.

- **1989** — Founded in Baudette, MN as a contract manufacturer.
- **2013** — Public listing on NASDAQ as ANIP.
- **2022** — Cortrophin® Gel launch — the only U.S.-based commercial repository corticotropin therapy with a competitive WAC.
- **2024** — Acquisition of Alimera Sciences, adding ILUVIEN® and YUTIQ® to the rare-disease portfolio.
