## Section — Benefits, eight categories

Card grid. Each card: category name, one-line summary, bullet list of what's included.

### Medical
Health plan options with company-funded HSA contributions for high-deductible plans.
- Health Savings Account (HSA) — company contributes
- Healthcare Flexible Spending Account (FSA)
- Dependent Care FSA

### Dental & Vision
Comprehensive coverage for employees and families.
- Preventive, restorative, and orthodontia
- Vision exams and lenses
- Discounts on eyewear and laser correction

### Insurance
Employer-paid baseline. Voluntary on top.
- Basic life insurance — paid by ANI
- AD&D — paid by ANI
- Supplemental life — voluntary

### Disability
Income protection for illness or injury.
- Short-term disability
- Long-term disability
- Permanent disability coverage

### Voluntary benefits
Add what you actually use.
- Accident insurance
- Critical illness
- Hospital indemnity
- Legal assistance
- Identity-fraud protection
- Pet insurance

### EAP (Employee Assistance Program)
24/7, confidential, for the whole household.
- Registered nurses
- Master's-level counselors
- Financial specialists
- Legal specialists

### Retirement & financial
Money in, money matched, money out.
- 401(k) with company match
- 100% vesting from day one
- Employee Stock Purchase Plan at a discount

### Time off
Time you don't have to ask for.
- 10 paid holidays
- Accruing PTO
- Parental leave
- Bereavement and jury duty pay
