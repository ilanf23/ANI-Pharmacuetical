## Section — Solid dose

**Where:** Baudette, MN — two separate sites.

**Granulation**
- Wet granulation
- Dry granulation
- Fluid bed (15 kg – 220 kg) and oven dry
- Direct compression

**Tablet dosage forms**
- Immediate release
- Modified release

**Tablet presses**
- Korsch XL200
- Courtoy R190
- Korsch XL400 (×3)
- Killian Synthesis 300

**Capsules**
- Hot melt fill — Bosch 1400 (×2)
- Immediate release — MG Suprema

**Coating**
- Sugar coating up to 100 kg pan load
- Aqueous film coating up to 400 kg pan load
- Solvent-based film coating up to 181 kg pan load

**Packaging**
- Tablets — round, capsule, elliptical · 12-count samples to 1,000-count bottles
- Capsules — size 00 to 3 · 30-count to 1,000-count bottles
- Additional configurations available with tooling
