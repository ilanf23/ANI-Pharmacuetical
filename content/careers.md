## Section — What you'd be joining

Three columns, one paragraph each. Each starts with a verb, not an adjective.

**Make.** Two manufacturing campuses in Baudette, MN run our solid- and liquid-dose lines. East Windsor, NJ runs R&D and specialty work. Operators, engineers, QA, supply chain — this is where the medicine actually gets made.

**Commercialize.** Princeton, NJ runs the Rare Disease and Generics commercial teams — sales, market access, medical affairs, marketing. The patient-facing work happens here.

**Operate.** Finance, legal, HR, IT, BD — the work that keeps an FDA-regulated public company on the rails.

## Section — Recruiting fraud notice

Direct, no defensive language.

**Headline:** Two minutes on hiring fraud.

We've seen scams that impersonate ANI. To protect yourself:

- Real ANI recruiters write from `@anipharmaceuticals.com` only.
- Real ANI recruiters have verified LinkedIn profiles.
- We never ask for payment, banking info, or sensitive financial data during hiring.
- All real openings live at `jobs.anipharmaceuticals.com`.
- If you got an offer that didn't follow this path, it isn't from us.

CTA: Report a suspicious job offer → `careers@anipharmaceuticals.com`
