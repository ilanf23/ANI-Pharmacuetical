**Subhead:**
Accessibility is a baseline at ANI, not a feature. If something isn't working for you, we want to hear about it.

### What we commit to
Five plain bullets:

- WCAG 2.2 AA conformance across pages we publish
- Keyboard-only navigation for every interaction
- Visible focus rings on every focusable element
- Captioned video for every published video asset
- Reduced-motion respect — all motion can be turned off via OS settings

## Design notes for all three legal pages

- Single-column layout, max content width 72ch.
- Body type: Inter 400 at body-md. Headings: Inter Tight 600 at heading-md and heading-lg.
- No hero image. Bone-200 surface. Indigo-500 link color, Saffron-700 link-hover.
- Footnote-style "last updated" date in `--font-eyebrow`, monospaced numerals.
- ISI bar does not appear on legal pages (no branded product claims).
