§01 / Our Story

Compounding quality, chapter by chapter.

Chapter 01 2001 — 2020

A generics manufacturer from the start.

ANI was founded as a U.S.-based generics maker in Baudette, Minnesota — a small town with a big bet: that disciplined manufacturing on American soil could compete on quality, price, and supply reliability for two decades and counting.

"We didn't set out to be the largest. We set out to make medicines we'd be willing to sign our names to." Founding principle, Baudette MN
Chapter 02 2020 — 2022

Cortrophin Gel returns — the first new ACTH option in 70 years.

In 2022, ANI relaunched Cortrophin® Gel: the first new repository corticotropin therapy approved in the United States in seven decades. A rare-disease franchise was built around it — and the company's center of gravity began to shift.

"One molecule. Eight indications. A market that had been served by a single supplier for 70 years." Source: ANI 2022 launch announcement
Chapter 03 2024 — 2025

ILUVIEN, YUTIQ, and a portfolio that compounds.

The 2024 acquisition of Alimera Sciences added ILUVIEN® and YUTIQ® to the rare-disease portfolio, deepening ANI's ophthalmology presence and bringing the rare-disease share of revenue toward 60%. By 2025, the rare-disease segment was no longer the future of the business — it was the present.

"Three FDA-inspected facilities. Two ophthalmology implants. Eight Cortrophin indications. One company writing the next chapter, not the same one." Source: ANI FY2025 annual report
Chapter 04 2026 →

Building the rare-disease company we were always becoming.

In 2026, a 90-person commercial team deploys mid-year for acute gouty arthritis flares. Cortrophin growth continues at +76% year-over-year. Generics and established brands keep funding the rare-disease pipeline. The "diversified" stays. The center has moved.

"When a condition is rare, our commitment can't be." ANI brand promise · Care, codified.

Indications & Usage

Cortrophin® Gel (repository corticotropin injection) is indicated as adjunctive therapy for short-term administration in conditions including acute exacerbations of multiple sclerosis, rheumatic disorders, collagen diseases, allergic states, ophthalmic diseases, respiratory diseases, edematous states (induction of diuresis or remission of proteinuria in nephrotic syndrome), and serum sickness.

ILUVIEN® (fluocinolone acetonide intravitreal implant) 0.19 mg is indicated for the treatment of diabetic macular edema in patients who have been previously treated with a course of corticosteroids and did not have a clinically significant rise in intraocular pressure, and for the treatment of chronic non-infectious uveitis affecting the posterior segment of the eye.
